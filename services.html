The foundation of everything

Groundworks

Every great project starts below the surface. Our groundworks team handles all excavation, drainage, levelling, and sub-base preparation in-house — ensuring every installation is built on ground that performs for decades. No shortcuts, no subcontractors.

  • Full site excavation and spoil removal
  • Land levelling, grading and compaction
  • Drainage design and installation
  • Sub-base preparation for patios, pools and driveways
  • Retaining walls and ground stabilisation
  • All groundworks carried out by our own team

Landscaping

We transform outdoor spaces from the ground up — designing and building gardens that are as functional as they are beautiful. From full renovations to turfing, planting, fencing, and feature walls, every element is handled with care and precision. Every project begins with a detailed 3D design so you see the finished result before we start.

  • Full garden design with 3D visualisations
  • Lawn installation and premium turfing
  • Feature walls, raised beds and borders
  • Fencing, screening and boundary work
  • Planting schemes and soft landscaping
  • Outdoor lighting integration

03
Precision stonework

Patios

A well-built patio is one of the highest-value investments you can make in your outdoor space. We install premium patios using the finest natural stone, porcelain, granite, and slate — all laid to the highest standard with proper drainage, precision jointing, and immaculate edges that stand the test of time.

  • Porcelain, natural stone, granite and slate
  • Full sub-base preparation and drainage
  • Precision cutting, laying and jointing
  • Steps, borders and edge detailing
  • Driveways, pathways and courtyards
  • Seating areas and entertainment zones

04
Your space, extended

Garden Rooms

Our bespoke garden rooms are designed to blend seamlessly with their surroundings while adding genuine living space to your property. Whether you need a home office, a gym, a games room, or a leisure retreat, we design and build the structure, foundation, and surrounding landscaping as one cohesive project — so everything ties together beautifully.

  • Bespoke design tailored to your garden
  • Full groundwork and foundation installation
  • Insulation, electrics and interior finishing
  • Bi-fold and sliding door options
  • Fully integrated with surrounding patio and landscaping
  • Home offices, gyms, leisure and entertainment rooms

05
The ultimate outdoor luxury

Swimming Pools

We handle every single element of a luxury pool installation — from the initial excavation through to the water going in. This includes all groundworks, structural build, pump and filtration systems, electrics and heating, surrounding paving, and landscaping. We also design and build integrated leisure rooms to house all pump and mechanical equipment, keeping everything concealed so your garden stays clean, uncluttered, and premium.

  • Full excavation and structural groundworks
  • Pump, filtration and heating systems installed in-house
  • Integrated leisure rooms to conceal all equipment
  • Underwater lighting and water feature installation
  • Hot tub and spa integration
  • Premium porcelain or stone pool surrounds
  • Full surrounding landscaping and outdoor lighting

How it works

Our process

A clear, transparent journey from first conversation to final handover — with no surprises along the way.

Step 01
Initial Consultation
We visit your site, discuss your vision, and assess the space. No obligation — just an honest conversation about what's possible.
Step 02
Design & Drawings
