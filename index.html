Our core services

Click any service to find out exactly what's involved — from the groundwork up.

Solid groundworks are the foundation of every great project — and it's where Greenscapes truly sets itself apart. We handle all excavation, levelling, drainage, and sub-base preparation in-house, ensuring every project is built on ground that will stand the test of time. No cutting corners, no outsourcing — just expert preparation done right.

  • Full site excavation and spoil removal
  • Land levelling and grading
  • Drainage installation and management
  • Sub-base compaction and preparation
  • Retaining walls and ground stabilisation
  • All groundworks carried out by our own team

We transform outdoor spaces from the ground up — designing and building gardens that are as functional as they are beautiful. From full garden renovations to turfing, planting, fencing, and feature walls, we handle every element with the same level of care and precision. Every project starts with a detailed design so you can visualise the result before we begin.

  • Full garden design with 3D visualisations
  • Lawn installation and turfing
  • Feature walls, raised beds and borders
  • Fencing, screening and boundary work
  • Planting schemes and soft landscaping
  • Outdoor lighting integration

A well-built patio is one of the highest-value investments you can make in your outdoor space. We install premium patios using the finest natural stone, porcelain, granite, and slate — all laid to the highest standard with proper drainage, precision jointing, and immaculate edges. The result is a space that looks exceptional and lasts for decades.

  • Porcelain, natural stone, granite and slate
  • Full sub-base preparation and drainage
  • Precision cutting and jointing
  • Steps, edges and border detailing
  • Driveways and pathways
  • Seating areas and entertainment zones

Our bespoke garden rooms are designed to blend seamlessly with their surroundings while adding genuine living space to your property. Whether you need a home office, a gym, a games room, or a leisure retreat, we design and build the structure, foundation, and surrounding landscaping as one cohesive project — so everything ties together perfectly from day one.

  • Bespoke design tailored to your garden
  • Full groundwork and foundation installation
  • Insulation, electrics and interior finishing
  • Bi-fold and sliding door options
  • Integrated with surrounding patio and landscaping
  • Home offices, gyms, leisure and entertainment rooms

We handle every single element of a luxury pool installation — from the initial excavation right through to the water going in. This includes all groundworks, structural build, pump and filtration systems, electric and heating, surrounding paving, and landscaping. We also design and build integrated leisure rooms to house all pump and mechanical equipment, keeping everything concealed and your garden looking clean, uncluttered, and premium.

  • Full excavation and structural groundworks
  • Pump, filtration and heating systems installed in-house
  • Integrated leisure rooms to conceal all equipment
  • Underwater lighting and water feature installation
  • Hot tub and spa integration
  • Premium porcelain or stone pool surrounds
  • Full landscaping and outdoor lighting around the pool
