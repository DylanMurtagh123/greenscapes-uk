Landscaping for Essex’s county city and its growing neighbourhoods

Chelmsford is Essex’s county town and a modern city with a mix of established residential roads, new developments, green spaces and surrounding villages. From Beaulieu and Channels to Springfield, Great Baddow, Writtle and Galleywood, each area needs a slightly different landscaping approach.

That is why a successful Chelmsford garden should feel polished, practical and long-lasting — designed for family life, home working, entertaining, privacy and the everyday pace of city living.

What Chelmsford gardens often need

  • Low-maintenance porcelain patios for busy family homes
  • Garden rooms for home offices, gyms and flexible living
  • Privacy planting and screening for overlooked gardens
  • Outdoor dining spaces connected properly to the house
  • Lighting that makes the garden usable after work and at weekends
  • Drainage and groundwork designed correctly beneath the finish

Our Process

A clear process from site visit to handover

Chelmsford landscaping projects often involve access, family use, drainage, privacy, modern materials and premium finishes. Our process keeps every stage clear.

Step 01

Chelmsford Site Visit

We assess access, levels, drainage, privacy, existing surfaces, boundaries and how the garden connects to the house.

Step 02

Design & Specification

We shape the layout, materials, planting, lighting and key features so you understand the project before work begins.

Step 03

Groundworks & Build

Our team handles preparation, drainage, sub-bases, paving, structures, garden rooms and the core landscaping installation.

Step 04

Detail & Handover

We finish the details, clean down the site and walk through the completed garden before final sign-off.
