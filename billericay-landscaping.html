Landscaping for leafy homes, family gardens and countryside-edge plots

Billericay has a strong residential feel with established family homes, green spaces and countryside edges. Norsey Wood sits on the outskirts of the town as a 175-acre local nature reserve, while Lake Meadows is one of Billericay’s best-loved public green spaces.

That local setting creates a clear opportunity for premium landscaping: gardens that feel calm and natural, but still work hard for modern living — outdoor dining, privacy, garden rooms, lawns, planting, lighting and year-round entertaining.
