Ingatestone Garden Specialists

Landscaping for a village with history, countryside and commuter appeal

Ingatestone has a rare mix of heritage character, countryside edges and practical London access. Ingatestone Hall stands close to the village in open countryside and retains its Tudor form, while the station provides direct trains to London Liverpool Street in around half an hour.

That means many local gardens need a more thoughtful approach than a standard patio-and-lawn package. The finish should feel premium, but also natural, sympathetic and practical for everyday living.

What Ingatestone gardens often benefit from

  • Natural stone and porcelain terraces that suit character homes
  • Garden rooms designed to feel part of the wider garden
  • Structured planting that softens patios and boundaries
  • Drainage and levels planned properly beneath the finish
  • Outdoor dining spaces for family life and entertaining
  • Lighting that feels subtle, warm and appropriate to the setting

Our Process

A clear process from site visit to handover

Ingatestone projects often involve older boundaries, levels, access, drainage, mature planting and careful material choices. Our process keeps every stage controlled.

Step 01

Ingatestone Site Visit

We assess access, levels, drainage, existing planting, boundaries, privacy and how the garden connects to the house.

Step 02

Design & Specification

Step 03

Step 04
