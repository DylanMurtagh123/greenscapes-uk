Who we are

More than just a landscaping company

Greenscapes UK was founded with a simple belief — that every client deserves a team that is completely focused on their project, from the very first conversation to the moment the final stone is laid.

We are a family run business operating out of Essex, and we're proud of the way we work. We take on one project at a time. No juggling, no split attention, no compromise. Just five skilled craftsmen giving everything they have to one client at a time.

From full garden renovations and premium patios to luxury swimming pools and bespoke garden rooms — we handle every aspect in-house, including design, groundworks, and the finishing details that other companies overlook.

Meet the founder

Stuart

"I started Greenscapes because I wanted to do things properly — no rushing, no cutting corners, no moving on to the next job before this one is perfect."

Stuart founded Greenscapes UK with a clear vision: build a landscaping company that puts quality above everything else. With over 15 years of hands-on experience in groundworks, hard landscaping, and pool installation, Stuart brings an unmatched level of technical knowledge to every project.

What makes Stuart different is that he doesn't just manage from a distance — he's on site, every day, working alongside the team and personally overseeing every detail. When you hire Greenscapes, you're not dealing with a salesperson. You're dealing directly with the person who will build your project.

Based in the heart of Essex and operating throughout the South East, Stuart and his team have built a reputation for exceptional results and a straightforward, honest approach to every client relationship.

What drives us

Our values

Everything we do is guided by a set of principles that haven't changed since day one.

Quality Without Compromise
We use only premium materials and proven methods. Every job is built to last — not just to look good on the day. We'd rather take longer and get it right than rush and cut corners.
Complete Focus
One project at a time — always. Your job gets our full team every day until it's done. That level of commitment is rare in this industry, and it's something we're incredibly proud of.
Honest & Transparent
We tell you exactly what's involved, what it will cost, and how long it will take — before we start. No hidden surprises, no vague promises. Just straight talking from start to finish.
Start-to-Finish Ownership
We handle everything — design, groundworks, build, and finishing — under one roof. No handoffs, no subcontractors, no excuses. One team, fully accountable, from first sketch to final stone.
Genuine Care
We care deeply about the spaces we create and the people we create them for. Your garden is an extension of your home — we treat it with the same respect we'd give our own.
Essex & Beyond
We're rooted in Essex but we go wherever the right project takes us. We've worked across the South East and beyond — and we bring the same dedication no matter where the job is.
